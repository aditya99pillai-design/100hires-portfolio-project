# AI-Powered SEO Content Production Research

## Chosen Topic

AI-Powered SEO Content Production

## Why I Chose This Topic

I have experience in SEO, content writing, and performance marketing, and I wanted to explore how AI is changing modern content production workflows.

AI is increasingly being used for keyword research, content planning, topical authority building, content generation, optimisation, and search performance analysis. Understanding how experienced practitioners integrate AI into these workflows is becoming essential for modern marketers.

The goal of this research project is to identify common frameworks, workflows, and best practices used by professionals working at the intersection of AI and SEO.

## Research Objective

This project aims to:

* Identify leading practitioners in AI-powered SEO
* Analyse their content, workflows, and recommendations
* Discover recurring patterns across experts
* Build a foundation for an AI SEO playbook based on real-world practices

## Research Methodology

For each expert, I collected recent content from LinkedIn, YouTube, and other publicly available sources. The objective was not simply to gather content, but to identify recurring themes, frameworks, and practical applications of AI within SEO and content production.

The findings from this research will be organised into individual expert profiles, content summaries, key takeaways, recurring patterns, and a consolidated AI SEO playbook.
