## Video Summary

### Core Argument

Nathan Gotch argues that businesses often overcomplicate AI SEO. Instead of chasing every new tactic, he recommends focusing on three daily activities that consistently improve visibility in both traditional search engines and AI-powered search platforms.

According to Nathan, AI SEO success comes from consistently improving existing content, publishing new intent-matched assets, and strengthening brand authority outside of a company's own website.

### The Three Daily Actions Framework

#### Daily Action #1: Optimise Existing Content

Nathan recommends reviewing existing content every day and improving pages that are already close to ranking well.

His process includes:

* Finding keywords ranking between positions 2 and 15 in Google Search Console
* Reviewing keyword placement in:

  * URL
  * Title tag
  * Meta description
  * H1
  * First sentence
* Expanding topical coverage
* Identifying missing entities and topic gaps
* Improving internal linking
* Strengthening topical authority

The goal is to improve pages that already have ranking potential rather than constantly creating new content.

#### Daily Action #2: Publish New SEO Assets

Nathan emphasises that businesses must continually expand their keyword footprint.

His workflow includes:

* Identifying keywords where existing pages rank poorly
* Determining whether search intent matches the existing page
* Creating dedicated assets for keywords with unique intent
* Producing:

  * Service pages
  * Category pages
  * Landing pages
  * Informational content
* Building content based on competitor research
* Maintaining a structured knowledge base
* Applying a consistent brand voice

He stresses that content should be grounded in real competitor data rather than relying entirely on AI-generated recommendations.

#### Daily Action #3: Improve Off-Site Authority

Nathan argues that on-site SEO alone is not enough for AI visibility.

Daily off-site activities should include:

* Entity optimisation
* Brand consistency management
* Citation research
* Outreach campaigns
* AI citation acquisition
* Brand mention opportunities
* Directory and profile clean-up

The objective is to improve how AI systems understand and trust a brand across the wider web.

### Entity SEO and AI Search

A significant portion of the video focuses on entity optimisation.

Nathan explains that AI systems build understanding through trusted sources across the web. If brand information is inconsistent, AI platforms may repeat inaccurate information to users.

Important entity signals include:

* Business name
* Address
* Phone number
* Brand descriptions
* Directory listings
* Social profiles

Maintaining consistency across these sources helps strengthen brand understanding within AI systems.

### AI Citation Strategy

Nathan highlights AI citations as an increasingly important ranking factor for AI-powered search environments.

His approach involves:

* Running commercial queries in ChatGPT
* Analysing Perplexity citations
* Reviewing Google AI Search responses
* Identifying commonly cited sources
* Finding citation gaps
* Pursuing placements on trusted retrieval sources

He notes that both linked and unlinked brand mentions can contribute to AI visibility.

### Content Production System

Nathan recommends building an AI-assisted content production system consisting of:

1. Competitor Research
2. Knowledge Base
3. Brand Voice Framework

This system helps ensure content remains relevant, differentiated, and aligned with business objectives.

## Key Takeaways

1. AI SEO should be treated as a daily operational process rather than a one-time project.

2. Existing content often provides the fastest SEO wins when keyword placement, topical coverage, and internal linking are improved.

3. Expanding keyword coverage requires publishing new assets that precisely match user intent.

4. AI visibility depends heavily on entity optimisation and brand consistency across the web.

5. AI citation acquisition is becoming increasingly important for visibility within ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and other AI search environments.

6. Businesses should develop knowledge bases and brand voice systems to improve AI-assisted content production.

7. Competitor-driven research remains critical even when using AI tools.

8. Off-site authority building continues to play a major role in both traditional SEO and AI SEO.

### Nathan Gotch's Recommended Daily AI SEO Workflow

* Optimise one existing asset per day
* Publish one new SEO asset per day
* Send 5–10 outreach emails per day or improve 5–10 brand properties per day

Nathan presents this workflow as a simple system that compounds over time and improves both search rankings and AI visibility.
