## Summary

Julian Goldie explains Google's rollout of Gemini inside Chrome, which is now available across approximately 172 countries and regions.

The update allows users to access Gemini through a side panel directly within Chrome instead of switching between separate AI applications.

Key capabilities include:

### 1. Content Summarisation

Gemini can instantly summarise long web pages and extract key information without requiring users to manually read entire articles.

### 2. Multi-Tab Analysis

Users can compare and analyse information across multiple tabs simultaneously, making research significantly faster.

### 3. Google App Integration

Gemini integrates directly with:

* Gmail
* Google Calendar
* Google Maps
* YouTube

This allows users to perform tasks without leaving their current browser tab.

### 4. AI Image Editing

Google's Nano Banana 2 image editing tool is integrated directly into Chrome, allowing users to modify images using prompts.

### 5. Personal Intelligence

Premium users can connect Gemini to personal Google services such as Gmail, YouTube, Search, and Google Photos to receive more personalised responses.
