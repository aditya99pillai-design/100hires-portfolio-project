### Summary

In this post, Mike King discusses how generative AI is fundamentally changing the way SEO performance should be measured. He explains that traditional metrics such as rankings and traffic were designed for search engines that presented users with lists of links. AI-powered search systems instead generate direct answers, reducing the importance of ranking positions and increasing the importance of being a trusted source that AI systems cite. Mike argues that businesses need to rethink their measurement frameworks and adapt to a search landscape where visibility, trust, and influence matter more than traditional ranking metrics.

### Key Takeaways

1. AI-powered search is reducing the importance of traditional ranking positions and click-based performance metrics.

2. Being cited and trusted by AI systems is becoming increasingly important for search visibility.

3. Businesses must develop new SEO measurement frameworks that account for AI influence and brand visibility.
