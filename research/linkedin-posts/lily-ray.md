### Summary

In this post, Lily Ray discusses the growing popularity of comparison pages and alternative pages as SEO and GEO tactics. These pages compare a company's products or services against competitors and are increasingly being produced at scale using AI. While acknowledging that such pages can provide value when created authentically, she warns that many organisations are generating large numbers of comparison pages without genuine experience using the products they review. Lily argues that Google, OpenAI, and other AI systems are increasingly prioritising objective third-party sources such as Reddit, YouTube reviews, Trustpilot, and G2 rather than relying on self-promotional brand content. She suggests that excessive scaling of AI-generated comparison pages may become a long-term risk as search engines and AI systems continue improving their ability to evaluate content quality and authenticity.

### Key Takeaways

1. Comparison and alternative pages have become popular SEO and GEO tactics, but large-scale implementation may create quality and spam concerns.

2. Authentic product experience and evidence remain important for review-style content, making AI-generated comparison content risky when it lacks genuine expertise.

3. AI search systems increasingly rely on independent third-party sources and reviews when evaluating brands and generating recommendations.

### Summary

In this post, Lily Ray shares a practical workflow for adapting traditional SEO keyword research to AI search behaviour. Using Google Sheets' AI functions, she demonstrates how marketers can convert keyword lists from SEO tools such as Ahrefs, Semrush, and Similarweb into natural-language prompts that users might ask AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or voice assistants. The workflow helps marketers understand how search intent translates into conversational AI queries and can be used to improve content planning for AI-driven search experiences.

### Key Takeaways

1. Traditional SEO keywords can be transformed into conversational AI prompts that better reflect how users interact with AI assistants and voice search tools.

2. AI-powered spreadsheet functions enable marketers to generate prompt variations at scale directly from keyword research datasets.

3. Understanding how keyword intent maps to conversational AI queries can help content teams create content better aligned with emerging AI search behaviour.
