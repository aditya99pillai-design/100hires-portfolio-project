Title:
AI Can't Trust What It Can't Understand. Schema Solves That Problem.

Summary:

In this post, Amanpreet explains why schema markup has become increasingly important in AI-driven search environments. He argues that modern AI systems do not simply evaluate keywords and backlinks but instead look for signals such as entities, context, relationships, credibility, and page purpose. The post outlines how different schema types help search engines and AI models understand website content more effectively. His core message is that websites that make their content easier for AI systems to interpret will have an advantage in AI-powered search.

Key Takeaways:

* Schema markup helps AI systems understand the meaning and context of content rather than just the words on a page.
* Different schema types serve different purposes, including establishing trust, credibility, identity, and user intent.
* Future AI search visibility will depend not only on content quality but also on how clearly that content is structured and explained.

Title:
Stop Chasing Rankings And Start Earning Attention

Summary:

In this post, Amanpreet challenges several outdated assumptions about SEO and argues that modern search marketing requires a broader approach than simply ranking for keywords. He highlights how AI-powered search experiences and changing buyer behaviour have altered the way users discover and evaluate information. Through a seven-point framework, he encourages marketers to focus on visibility, trust, demand generation, and business impact rather than traditional ranking metrics alone.

Key Takeaways:

* Ranking #1 is no longer the sole indicator of SEO success; visibility and attention across multiple search experiences matter more.
* Effective SEO involves content quality, audience understanding, authority building, and business outcomes, not just keywords and backlinks.
* AI is changing how users discover information, making it important for marketers to adapt their strategies rather than rely on outdated SEO practices.

Framework Covered In The Post:

1. Ranking #1 means we're winning
2. More content means more traffic
3. Keywords are the strategy
4. SEO is top-of-funnel only
5. Backlinks alone build authority
6. SEO success shows up only in monthly reports
7. AI will replace SEO anyway
