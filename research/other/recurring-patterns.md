# Recurring Patterns Across AI SEO Experts

## Overview

After analysing content from 10 experts across LinkedIn and YouTube, several recurring themes emerged regarding the future of SEO, AI-powered search, content production, and search visibility.

Although each expert approaches SEO from a different perspective, there is significant overlap in the strategies and trends they believe will shape the future of search.

---

## Pattern 1: Traditional Rankings Are No Longer Enough

Several experts highlighted that ranking #1 in Google is no longer the sole measure of search success.

AI-powered search experiences such as ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, and other answer engines increasingly provide direct answers without requiring users to click through to websites.

### Experts Supporting This View

* Neil Patel
* Mike King
* Amanpreet Singh Gulati
* Nathan Gotch
* Julian Goldie

### Key Insight

SEO success is shifting from pure rankings toward overall visibility across AI-powered search environments.

---

## Pattern 2: AI Citations Are Becoming A New Visibility Metric

Many experts discussed the growing importance of being referenced or cited by AI systems.

Rather than simply ranking for keywords, brands must become trusted sources that AI systems use when generating answers.

### Experts Supporting This View

* Neil Patel
* Mike King
* Aleyda Solis
* Lily Ray

### Key Insight

Future SEO measurement may increasingly include AI citations, mentions, and visibility within AI-generated responses.

---

## Pattern 3: Entity Optimisation Matters More Than Ever

Experts repeatedly emphasised the importance of helping search engines and AI systems understand entities, relationships, and context.

Schema markup, structured data, brand consistency, and clear topical associations help AI systems better understand content.

### Experts Supporting This View

* Amanpreet Singh Gulati
* Neil Patel
* Nathan Gotch
* Aleyda Solis

### Key Insight

AI systems reward content that is easy to interpret, classify, and connect to known entities.

---

## Pattern 4: Topical Authority Remains Critical

Despite rapid changes in AI search, experts consistently agreed that topical authority remains one of the strongest long-term SEO advantages.

Building comprehensive coverage around a topic continues to outperform isolated content production.

### Experts Supporting This View

* Koray Tugberk GUBUR
* Matt Diggity
* Nathan Gotch
* Himani Kankaria

### Key Insight

AI systems and search engines continue to favour websites that demonstrate deep expertise across an entire subject area.

---

## Pattern 5: GEO Is Emerging As An Extension Of SEO

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) appeared repeatedly across the research.

Experts increasingly discuss optimisation for AI-generated answers alongside traditional search engine optimisation.

### Experts Supporting This View

* Himani Kankaria
* Aleyda Solis
* Amanpreet Singh Gulati
* Mike King

### Key Insight

Modern SEO is evolving into a broader discipline that includes visibility within AI-generated search experiences.

---

## Pattern 6: Content Quality And Trust Signals Are Becoming More Important

Several experts stressed that AI systems increasingly rely on trust signals when selecting sources.

Third-party mentions, reviews, citations, expertise, and content quality all contribute to visibility.

### Experts Supporting This View

* Lily Ray
* Neil Patel
* Mike King
* Aleyda Solis

### Key Insight

Trust and authority are becoming increasingly important as AI systems determine which sources deserve visibility.

---

## Conclusion

The research suggests that AI is not replacing SEO. Instead, it is expanding SEO into a broader discipline focused on visibility, authority, trust, topical expertise, and AI discoverability.

The strongest future SEO strategies will combine traditional optimisation techniques with AI-focused approaches such as entity optimisation, citation building, GEO, and topical authority development.
