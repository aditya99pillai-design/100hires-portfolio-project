# AI SEO Playbook

## Overview

After analysing content from 10 SEO professionals across LinkedIn and YouTube, several recurring patterns emerged regarding how AI is changing SEO and content production.

While individual experts have different approaches, there is strong agreement on the direction search is moving. Traditional SEO remains important, but visibility within AI-powered search systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Bing Copilot is becoming increasingly important.

This document summarises the most common recommendations and frameworks identified during the research process.

---

# 1. Build Topical Authority Instead of Isolated Content

Experts Referenced:

* Koray Tugberk GUBUR
* Nathan Gotch
* Matt Diggity
* Himani Kankaria

Key Insight:

AI systems favour websites that demonstrate expertise across an entire topic rather than isolated articles targeting individual keywords.

Recommended Actions:

* Create topic clusters around core business themes.
* Build supporting content around primary topics.
* Interlink related content extensively.
* Maintain consistent publishing within chosen topical areas.

---

# 2. Optimise For Entities And Context

Experts Referenced:

* Koray Tugberk GUBUR
* Amanpreet Singh Gulati
* Aleyda Solis

Key Insight:

Modern search systems increasingly rely on entities, relationships, context, and semantic understanding rather than simple keyword matching.

Recommended Actions:

* Implement structured data and schema markup.
* Clearly identify people, products, brands, services, and locations.
* Build content that explains relationships between entities.
* Improve contextual relevance throughout content.

---

# 3. Prioritise AI Search Visibility

Experts Referenced:

* Aleyda Solis
* Mike King
* Himani Kankaria
* Lily Ray
* Neil Patel

Key Insight:

Ranking in Google alone is no longer sufficient.

Brands must also be discoverable within AI-generated responses.

Recommended Actions:

* Track citations in AI-generated answers.
* Monitor AI Overview appearances.
* Improve brand mentions across authoritative sources.
* Build content that AI systems can easily reference.

---

# 4. Build Trust Through Third-Party Validation

Experts Referenced:

* Lily Ray
* Neil Patel
* Mike King

Key Insight:

AI systems frequently rely on third-party validation when evaluating brands and content quality.

Recommended Actions:

* Earn mentions on authoritative websites.
* Encourage reviews on trusted platforms.
* Build digital PR campaigns.
* Develop brand authority beyond owned media.

---

# 5. Use AI To Increase Content Production Efficiency

Experts Referenced:

* Matt Diggity
* Nathan Gotch
* Julian Goldie
* Neil Patel

Key Insight:

AI is most effective when used to accelerate workflows rather than fully replace human expertise.

Recommended Actions:

* Use AI for research and outlines.
* Use AI for content planning.
* Use AI for content summarisation.
* Maintain human review before publishing.

---

# 6. Measure Business Outcomes Instead Of Rankings Alone

Experts Referenced:

* Himani Kankaria
* Amanpreet Singh Gulati
* Mike King

Key Insight:

Success should be measured through business impact rather than rankings alone.

Recommended Actions:

* Track leads and revenue.
* Measure branded search growth.
* Monitor AI visibility metrics.
* Evaluate content influence across the customer journey.

---

# 7. Keep Content Fresh

Experts Referenced:

* Neil Patel
* Aleyda Solis
* Nathan Gotch

Key Insight:

Freshness continues to influence both traditional search engines and AI retrieval systems.

Recommended Actions:

* Update high-performing content regularly.
* Refresh statistics and examples.
* Expand articles as industries evolve.
* Monitor content decay over time.

---

# Core Conclusion

The strongest pattern across all researched experts is that SEO is evolving from ranking optimisation into visibility optimisation.

Success increasingly depends on:

* Topical authority
* Entity understanding
* Trust signals
* AI visibility
* Content quality
* Brand recognition

Traditional SEO fundamentals remain important, but businesses must also adapt to AI-powered search experiences if they want to remain visible as search behaviour continues to evolve.
